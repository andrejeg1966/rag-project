# User Handbook for the Customer Portal

The customer portal bundles everything to do with your user account: sign-in,
password management, billing, support and your personal settings. This handbook
describes each area and the rules that apply there. The ten chapters below walk
through the features you use most often in the portal, in the order you are
likely to meet them.

1. Signing in

Access to the customer portal is protected by an email address and a personal
password. You enter both on the sign-in page; after that the portal is fully
available to you. For security reasons, every account is locked temporarily
after several failed sign-in attempts, so that nobody can guess the password by
trying. The lock lifts automatically; there is no need to notify support.

- Sign-in with email address and password
- After three failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes
- The lock lifts automatically

1.1 How do I recognise a lock?

When an account is locked, a notice with the remaining wait time appears on the
sign-in attempt. Wait that time out and then sign in again. If you have
forgotten the password in the meantime, chapter 2 will help.

2. Resetting your password

If you no longer know your password, a "Forgot password" link is available on
the sign-in page. After selecting the link you enter the email address stored on
your account. The system then sends a message containing a link through which
you can set a new password. That link is deliberately short-lived: it expires
after 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, please
check your spam folder before starting the process again.

- "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page
- Enter the email address of the account
- The reset link is valid for 30 minutes

2.1 What happens once the link has expired?

After the deadline the link leads to an error message. Simply request a new link
through the same page; there is no limit on how often you may do so.

3. Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication secures your account further. In addition to the
password, the portal then asks for a second proof that an attacker cannot simply
read off. The feature is enabled in the account settings. The options are
authenticator apps on your smartphone and one-time codes sent by email. Setting
up an authenticator app is considered the safer variant, because the code never
leaves the device.

- Enabled in the account settings
- Authenticator apps and one-time codes by email are supported
- Recommendation: use an authenticator app

3.1 What if the second device is lost?

If the smartphone is lost, signing in is not possible at first. In that case
contact support, who reset the two-factor authentication after an identity
check.

4. Invoices

You will find all invoices for your contract in the "Billing" menu. They are
available there to download as PDF files, so that you can save, print or pass
them on to your accounting department. Invoices remain available in the customer
portal for five years. Download important receipts in good time if you want to
keep them longer.

- "Billing" menu
- Download as a PDF file
- Retention in the portal: five years

4.1 Can I still find older invoices?

After the five years have passed, invoices are removed from the portal. If you
need an older receipt, support can usually supply it for a processing fee.

5. Support

Support is there to help with questions and faults. It is reachable Monday to
Friday from 08:00 to 18:00. The easiest way to submit a request is through the
contact form in the customer portal; that way your account details are already
on file and processing is faster. Requests outside service hours are handled on
the next working day.

- Service hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00
- Contact form in the customer portal
- Requests outside those hours are handled on the next working day

5.1 Which details help with a request?

Please state your customer number, the area concerned and a short description in
the form. Screenshots speed up the resolution of technical problems.

6. Editing your user profile

You maintain your personal details in the "My profile" menu. There you can
change your name, phone number and preferred language. The preferred language
determines the language in which you receive notifications and notices from the
portal. For security reasons a change of email address does not take effect
immediately: the system sends a confirmation link to the new address, valid for
24 hours. Only after confirmation is the change applied.

6.1 Why is the email address confirmed separately?

The email address serves both as your sign-in name and as the recipient of
security notices. Confirmation makes sure the address really belongs to you and
that a typo has not addressed someone else's mailbox.

7. Notifications

In the "Notifications" menu you decide which messages you want to receive. The
options are email notifications and notices inside the portal; both channels can
be configured independently of each other. Security-relevant messages, for
instance about sign-ins or password changes, cannot be fully disabled. This
exception protects your account: you should notice if somebody else tries to
gain access to it.

- Choice between email and portal notifications
- Both channels configurable independently
- Security-relevant messages cannot be fully disabled

7.1 What counts as a security-relevant message?

This includes notices about sign-ins from new devices, about password and email
changes, and about account locks. These messages always reach you through the
contact channel on file.

8. Uploading documents

In the "Documents" area you can store your own files and make them available to
support or case handlers. The formats PDF, PNG and JPG are supported; the
maximum file size is 10 MB. Please split larger files or convert them into a
more compact PDF. Uploaded documents can be deleted again within 30 days. After
that the system removes them automatically.

- Formats: PDF, PNG and JPG
- Maximum file size: 10 MB
- Deletion possible within 30 days

8.1 Why is my document not accepted?

It is usually the file size or the format. Check the details above the file
picker; for a different format, convert the file first, for instance with a PDF
export from your word processor.

9. Deactivating your user account

If you no longer wish to use the portal, the account can be deactivated under
"Account settings". After deactivation signing in is no longer possible, but the
stored data is retained at first. Within 14 days support can restore the account
if you change your mind. Once that period has passed the account is deleted for
good; restoration is then no longer possible.

- Deactivation under "Account settings"
- No sign-in after deactivation
- Restoration by support within 14 days
- After that, final deletion

9.1 What happens to my invoices when the account is deleted?

Deleting the account for good also removes access to the invoices stored in the
portal. Download receipts you still need beforehand.

10. Data protection and session duration

The portal ends a session automatically after 20 minutes without activity. This
protection prevents an unattended computer from leaving access open. If you work
on a public or shared device, sign out manually as well. You can export your
personal data in the data protection area. Depending on the volume of stored
details, producing the export can take up to 24 hours; you will be notified as
soon as the data is ready.

- Automatic sign-out after 20 minutes without activity
- Data export available in the data protection area
- Producing the export takes up to 24 hours

10.1 For which reasons is a session ended?

Beyond inactivity, a session ends when you sign out, when the browser is closed
in some configurations, and on a password change. In every case signing in again
is required.

Glossary

- Customer portal: the web application in which you manage your account.
- Session: the period between signing in and signing out.
- Two-factor authentication: signing in with a password and a second proof.
- Authenticator app: a program on your smartphone that generates one-time codes.
